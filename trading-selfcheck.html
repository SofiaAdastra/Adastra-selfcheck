The pattern in your continuous-evidence answers

Each of those questions is easy to answer for an ideal shift and hard to answer for a typical one under pressure. A desk can be fast in the moment and still unable to reconstruct why a decision was right, or even what it was, a week later.

That gap does not close by trading faster. It closes when speed and defensibility are produced by the same step: the position impact, the price context, and the decision record generated together, from one fixed snapshot, at the moment the decision is made — not reconstructed afterward from memory and separate systems.
